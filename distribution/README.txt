FOLD - The Quiet Dimension

A four-dimensional puzzle platformer with five floating gardens.
Collect each garden's echoes, then reach the amber gate. Hold Q or E to
rotate your three-dimensional slice through the fourth spatial dimension.

PLAY

Extract the entire archive before playing. Keep Fold.pck beside the executable.
You do not need to install Godot.

Windows (64-bit Intel/AMD): double-click Fold.exe.
Linux (64-bit Intel/AMD): run ./Fold.x86_64 from the extracted directory.
If your extractor removed executable permissions, run chmod +x Fold.x86_64.
The game uses OpenGL Compatibility rendering and requires working graphics
drivers and a desktop display. A keyboard and mouse are required.

CONTROLS

Mouse             Look around
WASD / arrows     Move relative to the camera
Space             Jump
Hold Q / E        Rotate the slice in either direction
H                 Next hint
R                 Restart the current garden
Escape            Pause / resume; choose a chapter or quit from the menu
M                 Mute / unmute
Enter             Start / continue

Choose "Jump the 4D shapes" or "Explore the labyrinth" on the title screen,
or choose any of the five gardens from the pause menu.

Progress lasts for the current session only. The labyrinth and folded ascent
save echo checkpoints during play. Falling returns to your latest checkpoint;
restarting clears the garden. Closing the game does not save progress.
